Sales and revenue

Revenue Farm: live sales leaderboard

Live

A big-screen leaderboard on the sales-floor TV. It builds itself from the reps' own sales posts in the team chat, so nobody types anything twice.

Explore the build +
Problem
  • Sales were posted as free-text messages in a group chat, and totals were tallied by hand.
  • No live view of who was winning the month, so there was no competitive energy.
What it does
  • A parsing engine reads every post, pulls out product and amount, credits the poster, and updates the board within minutes.
  • Animated sale celebrations with custom sounds, a rank change takeover, and milestone events.
  • Separate counters for memberships, reviews and referrals, plus a rotating Hall of Fame of past monthly winners.
  • The board rolls over on the 1st of each month by itself and archives the closed month.
Hard parts solved
  • Messy human input: two deals in one message, totals restated in follow-ups, product names with numbers in them (a product like "BPC-157" is not $157), and even amounts written in Japanese numerals.
  • Dedup against the CRM so a sale is never counted twice.
  • A browser audio limit that went silent after about 6 sales, fixed with a single shared audio context.
Privacy design
  • The board shows rep, product and dollar amount only. Patient names never leave the parser.
  • A file allowlist on hosting means only the public data file is ever served, so internal logs stay private.

Stack: Python parsing engine on a schedule, static HTML/JS board on Vercel, Web Audio, serverless API routes, Vercel Blob.

Sales and revenue

Instagram lead router

Live

A setter texts a screenshot of an interested Instagram lead. The lead gets booked, the rep gets assigned, and the team gets the alert, with no data entry.

Explore the build +
Flow
  • Reads the phone number and name off the screenshot with OCR.
  • Finds or creates the contact in the CRM, with no duplicates.
  • Books straight into a round-robin consult calendar, or texts a self-booking link.
  • Sends the patient a confirmation that names their rep, and posts an alert in the sales chat with the time.
Edge cases handled
  • Existing patients go to their own rep first. If that rep is booked, the lead falls back to anyone free rather than double-booking.
  • If no rep has the slot, the patient is told right away and a coordinator gets a call task.
  • Follow-ups only fire during business hours. Off-hours pings wait until the next morning.

Stack: Python, vision OCR, CRM REST API (contacts, calendars, conversations).

Reactivation outreach engine

LiveHanded to the office AI agent and launched

A campaign engine that wins back past leads who never bought. It ranks them, assigns them to reps, and sends on a controlled schedule.

Explore the build +
Build
  • Audited and ranked a large past-lead file hot to cold. The file was handled in deidentified form.
  • Five-email sequences, spread across several rep mailboxes to protect deliverability, in batches with time-of-day guards.
  • 76 automated tests passing before any live send.
After launch
  • Open, click and unsubscribe tracking written back to the CRM as tags. Unsubscribe is honored automatically.
  • Bounce parsing from returned mail, with automatic list scrubbing and a circuit breaker that pauses sending if the bounce rate crosses a threshold.
  • Round two re-segmented by behavior (never opened, opened, clicked). Warm groups got plain personal emails to reach the primary inbox instead of the promotions tab.
  • A booking watcher tells the team when someone from the campaign books.

Stack: Python, Gmail API with domain-wide delegation, Vercel tracking endpoints, CRM API.

Website intake to CRM sync

Live

Every intake and purchase on the new website lands in the CRM, attached to the right patient and the right rep.

Explore the build +
What it does
  • A Salesforce Flow pushes each new intake and purchase to the CRM. The contact is created or updated, a cross-system ID is stored, and intake answers go into a note.
  • An owner-assignment rule runs every 5 minutes. Existing patients keep their rep. A new patient goes to the rep who actually took their consult. If nobody did, it's flagged for a manager instead of guessed.

Why it mattered
  • The source system assigned the wrong rep, so I made the CRM the source of truth for ownership and wrote the rule to match how the floor actually works.

02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE BUILDS

Lead the people.
Solve the problem.
Bring the plan home.

Military service taught me to think on my feet, make decisions under pressure, and turn a good idea into a plan that gets finished. I brought that mindset into civilian healthcare at Florida Orthopaedic Institute, where I grew as an operations leader.

Near the end of my time there, I began using AI to work through large files, support recruiting, and check that important details weren’t lost in major updates. At Overtime Men’s Health, that grew into a hands-on approach to building: limited budgets, limited tools, and operational problems that needed answers in days.

I take ownership wherever I can see a useful solution. I bring ideas to leadership, check feasibility, and iterate with the team until the tool fits the way they work. I scope the problem, direct AI coding agents, and own the design and release decisions.

“If I build a beautiful dashboard that no one looks at because it’s clunky or missing the information they need, what’s the point?”

The same principle guides how I lead: help people grow their skills, build their careers, and give them systems that make their work easier.
